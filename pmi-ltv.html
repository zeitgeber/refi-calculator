Loan-to-value and mortgage insurance

Published by zeitgeber · Last updated August 7, 2026

What LTV means

Loan-to-value, or LTV, is loan balance divided by home value. For example, a $320,000 balance on a $400,000 home is 80% LTV. It is a measure of equity, not a home appraisal or approval decision.

Mortgage insurance can affect the payment. CFPB notes conventional loans commonly require PMI below 20% down, while FHA and USDA loans typically have mortgage insurance. Exact rules depend on the loan program and lender.

Refinance implication

A new appraisal or principal reduction can change LTV. Confirm whether mortgage insurance will remain, end, or change before accepting a quote; do not rely on a generic threshold.
